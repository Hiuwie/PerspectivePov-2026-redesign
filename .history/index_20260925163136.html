Before You Start

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Know what to expect before committing to a website.

Ask us about your project →
How much does a business website cost?

Our Starter websites begin at R2,500, Business at R5,500, and Professional at R9,500. Your final quote depends on the content, pages, and features needed. We confirm the scope and cost before work begins.

How quickly can our website go live?

A simple Starter site can be live in about a week once your content is ready and the scope is agreed. Standard websites typically take 3 to 5 weeks; custom projects may take longer. We confirm your timeline in the quote.

Are hosting and a domain included?

Hosting and domain costs are separate from website design and development. We can help with setup or work with your existing provider, and explain the ongoing costs before launch.

Will our website work on mobile and appear on Google?

We build responsive websites and include SEO foundations such as clear headings, page titles, descriptions, and optimised images. These help search engines understand your site. Indexing and rankings are not guaranteed; ongoing content and SEO may be needed.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. We offer redesigns, migrations, content updates, and fixes. Send us your current website address and what is not working for your business so we can scope the changes.

What happens after launch?

We include 30 days of support for bug fixes and launch-related adjustments. Monthly maintenance and one-off updates are available separately. See our support and maintenance options.
