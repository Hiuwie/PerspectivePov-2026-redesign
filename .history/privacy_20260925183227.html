Who we are

Perspective Point of view provides web design, development, and maintenance services in South Africa. You can contact us at design@perspectivepov.co.za.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the name, email address, package preference, and project message that you submit. We also receive campaign information such as referral URLs and advertising parameters so we can understand which campaigns generate enquiries.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, prepare quotes, provide services, and maintain business records. We use optional analytics and advertising tools only when you accept marketing cookies.

Analytics and advertising

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to measure visits, campaign performance, quote-form submissions, and WhatsApp link clicks. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. You can decline optional tracking using the consent banner.

Service providers

Enquiries are sent by email to Perspective Point of view. The site may also load third-party services such as Bootstrap, Google Fonts, Google Analytics, Meta, and the SociableKIT Instagram feed when those services are enabled.

Retention and your rights

We retain enquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for responding, business administration, legal obligations, and resolving disputes. You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction, or ask us to delete it where applicable.

Contact
