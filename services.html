Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Do you offer ongoing maintenance packages?

+

Yes. Perspective offers flexible maintenance plans tailored to different needs:

  • Basic (R1,500/month): regular CMS & plugin updates, weekly backups, and basic security monitoring.
  • Standard (R3,000/month): everything in Basic + monthly performance checks, small content updates (up to 2 hrs), and priority support.
  • Premium (Custom): all Standard features + monthly analytics review, A/B test support, or dedicated SLA for larger sites.
We also offer ad-hoc support at an hourly rate. Maintenance plans can be started after launch and are billed monthly.

Can you migrate my existing website?

+

Absolutely. Our migration process is designed to protect content and SEO:

  1. Discovery: we audit the current site, plugins, URLs and data to plan the migration.
  2. Backup & Staging: full backups are taken and a staging site is prepared for testing.
  3. Migration: content, media, and URLs are migrated; redirects are created where needed.
  4. Testing & Go-live: we test functionality and performance then perform DNS cutover with minimal downtime.
We pay special attention to SEO (301 redirects, canonical tags, sitemaps) to preserve search rankings. Costs depend on site size and complexity; we provide a migration estimate after the audit.

Do you provide hosting and domain registration?

+

Yes — we can handle hosting and domain setup, or work with your existing provider:

  • We recommend managed hosting providers for WordPress and e-commerce (fast PHP, CDN, automatic backups).
  • We can register domains on your behalf or assist with transfers; DNS changes are handled during launch.
  • If you prefer, we’ll provide a hosting spec and you can purchase hosting directly; we’ll still configure and deploy the site.
Typical hosting fees are separate from our development costs; we’ll advise on the best option for your traffic and budget.

How many revision rounds are included?

+

We separate design and development phases to keep timelines clear:

  • Design phase: unlimited design revisions until you approve the final mockups — we won’t move to development until you’re happy.
  • Development phase: includes 2 rounds of content/bug-fix revisions after the site is built. Additional revisions are billed at our hourly rate or included in maintenance plans.
This approach keeps projects predictable while still giving you control over the final outcome.

Can you build custom features or integrations?

+

Yes. Perspective builds custom integrations and features — common examples include:

  • Payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal)
  • CRM integrations (HubSpot, Zoho)
  • Email marketing (Mailchimp, Klaviyo)
  • Booking & membership systems
Custom work is scoped and quoted after a short discovery call. We provide a clear estimate with milestones and deliverables so you know exactly what you’re getting.

What if I need changes after launch?

+

After launch Perspective includes a 30-day complimentary support window for bug fixes and launch-related adjustments. For ongoing changes we offer:

  • Monthly maintenance plans (for regular updates and small changes)
  • Hourly rates for one-off feature requests or larger edits
  • Priority response available on Premium plans
If you anticipate frequent updates, we recommend choosing a maintenance plan to keep costs predictable.
